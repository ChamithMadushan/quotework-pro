# Product requirements: QuoteCraft

## Vision

QuoteCraft is a fast, mobile-friendly quotation generator for small businesses, freelancers, contractors, and suppliers. The first market is Sri Lanka (LKR). International currencies are available in the form so later markets can reuse the same product.

## Principles

- Basic quoting is free and does not require registration.
- Privacy by default: drafts stay in the browser.
- Do not invent business, customer, tax, or price data.
- Optional fields are labeled.
- Calculations must be consistent in the form, preview, print view, and PDF.
- Do not claim legal or tax compliance.

## Users

SMEs, retail and wholesale, IT and CCTV suppliers, construction contractors, freelancers, agencies, repair services, furniture and textile businesses, and procurement teams.

## In scope (v1)

Business and customer details, logo upload, multi-item quotations, item and document discounts, optional tax line, currency and dates, unique quotation number, validity date, notes and terms, live preview, PDF download, print, edit/reset, local drafts, responsive layout.

## Out of scope (v1)

Accounts, database, email sending, e-signatures, inventory, multi-user teams, payment collection, automatic IRD/VAT filing, OCR, and mobile native apps.

## Success criteria

A first-time user can produce a branded LKR quotation PDF in under five minutes without creating an account.
